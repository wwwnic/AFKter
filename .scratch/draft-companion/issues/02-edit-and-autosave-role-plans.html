02: Edit and autosave role plans live

Status
ready-for-agent
Blocked by
None

Summary

The player can build and reorder pick and ban priorities for each role, with immediate effective changes, automatic persistence, and visible save failure status.

Source: approved draft companion design.

Acceptance criteria

  • Provide a modest native plan editor with champion identification, ordered lists, empty plans, and no Apply button. Record the champion catalog source and update/failure behavior.
  • Publish one immutable revision per complete edit; do not publish unfinished drags or invalid input. Persist coherent revisions without delaying their in-memory effect.
  • Expose current effective revision and persistence health through the UI and existing diagnostics.
  • Failed saving leaves the latest in-memory plan effective and visibly unsaved; retry and restart recovery are verified. Malformed stored data cannot create arbitrary plans.
  • Demonstrate rapid editing, reorder, failure/recovery, and reopening the app. Verify these behaviors without a League client.
