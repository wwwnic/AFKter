Summary

Provide the domain operations and offline role data needed by the new editor, preserving existing plans, policy, timing and autosave.

Source: approved design · implementation plan.

Acceptance criteria

  • Insert, reorder and remove use stable champion identity against the current list. A completed edit publishes one coherent revision; stale, invalid, unchanged and duplicate operations do not publish partial changes.
  • Copying to another role or pick/ban list preserves the source. Duplicate destinations identify their existing entry without changing order. Missing reorder sources or insertion anchors are rejected safely.
  • Bundle role metadata from the user-owned reference, translate role names correctly, preserve attribution, and verify coverage of all 173 current champion IDs and 281 memberships.
  • Missing or invalid role metadata does not corrupt plans or identity browsing. Unmapped champions remain available under All/search, and filters never restrict plan choices.
  • Focused behavioral tests and the existing core suite verify revision, settings, recovery and persistence compatibility.
