Summary

Run AFKtor as a Windows tray app and see whether it is connected to League as the client starts, exits, or restarts.

AFKtor v1 spec

Acceptance criteria

  • Start AFKtor with League closed. The tray shows that League is disconnected.
  • Launch League and verify that AFKtor detects it and shows connected after establishing a local LCU connection.
  • Exit and relaunch League. The tray shows disconnected and then connected again without restarting AFKtor.
